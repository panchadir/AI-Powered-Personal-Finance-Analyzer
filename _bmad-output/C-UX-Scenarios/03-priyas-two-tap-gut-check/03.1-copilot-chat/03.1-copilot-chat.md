## Overview

**Page Purpose:** Priya gets a two-tap, honest gut-check on a real ₹1,200 dinner decision, with the reasoning attached.

**Entry Context:** Already a habitual user (~2 weeks in), she opens the app directly to the Copilot right after getting a dinner invite, mid-conversation with her colleague.

**Exit Action:** None — this is the final (and only) step of the scenario. She gets an honest "yes, that's fine" with reasoning and returns to her evening, guilt-free. ✓

**On-Page Interactions:**
- Taps the "Can I afford ₹___?" gut-check quick-prompt button
- Enters ₹1,200 and receives an honest yes/maybe/not-today with reasoning traced to her real Safe-to-Spend

## User Situation (Q3)

Priya — The Overwhelmed Earner (Primary). About two weeks into using the app, mid-afternoon at work, a colleague invites her to dinner that will cost around ₹1,200.

## Mental State (Q4)

**Hope:** Get an instant, honest answer so she can just say yes and enjoy the evening.

**Worry:** Guessing wrong — either overspending into next week's cushion, or needlessly declining out of unfounded fear.

## Discovery Method (Q6)

Already a habitual return user — opens the app directly to the Copilot the moment she gets the invite, no discovery step needed.
