## Overview

**Page Purpose:** Deliver a two-tap, five-second honest answer to a real spending decision — "Can I afford ₹1,200 for dinner tonight?" — so Priya can say yes or no without guilt or second-guessing.

**User Situation:** Priya is ~2 weeks into using the app. She's mid-conversation with a colleague about a dinner invite. She has about 20 seconds of attention to give the app before she needs to respond. Speed and clarity are everything. A hesitant, conditional, or over-qualified answer will frustrate her; an invented number she can't verify will destroy the trust she's built.

**Success Criteria:** Priya taps the "Can I afford ₹___?" gut-check quick prompt, enters ₹1,200, receives an honest answer with 1-2 sentences of reasoning traced to her real Safe-to-Spend, and exits the app with a clear, guilt-free decision in under 10 seconds.

**Entry Points:**
- Bottom nav "Copilot" tab — direct access from any page in the app
- Home screen bookmark / PWA shortcut

**Exit Points:**
- Close app / navigate away — answer received, scenario complete ✓

### Override: Copilot Response — Gut-Check Mode

When the gut-check quick prompt is used, the Copilot response is **constrained to gut-check mode**:

**Response structure (gut-check mode):**

1. **Verdict first** — one of three states:
   - ✅ "Yes, that's fine." — amount is well within Safe-to-Spend
   - ⚠️ "It's a stretch." — amount is 50–100% of current Safe-to-Spend
   - 🔴 "Not today." — amount exceeds Safe-to-Spend

2. **Reasoning** — 1–2 sentences, specific to her real data. Example: "Your Safe-to-Spend is ₹2,840 right now, so ₹1,200 for dinner leaves you ₹1,640 for the rest of the week — comfortably within your usual week-end spend."

3. **Data trace** — same chips as 01.7, always present

**Response length constraint:** Gut-check responses must be ≤ 3 sentences total. The system prompt for gut-check mode must enforce brevity.

## Technical Notes

- **Gut-check mode is a server-side instruction** — the `message` sent to `/copilot/chat` includes a `mode: 'gut_check'` field when triggered from the quick-prompt bar; this switches the system prompt to the brevity-constrained gut-check variant
- **Verdict badge from structured output** — the API response for gut-check mode returns a structured header `{ verdict: 'yes' | 'stretch' | 'no', text: '...' }` before the streaming text so the badge renders immediately
- **Safe-to-Spend pre-context injection** — when the gut-check bar is used, the current Safe-to-Spend figure is injected into the system context automatically (from the most recent dashboard fetch), so the Copilot doesn't need to recalculate it or ask for it
- **Response must be honest even when the verdict is "No"** — "Not today" must never be framed as judgment ("you've been spending too much") but as a simple factual limit ("₹1,200 would exceed your Safe-to-Spend of ₹980 today"). The honesty moat applies equally to unfavourable answers.
