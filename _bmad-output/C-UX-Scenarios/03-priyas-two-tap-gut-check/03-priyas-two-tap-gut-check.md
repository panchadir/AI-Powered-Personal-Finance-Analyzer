# 03: Priya's Two-Tap Gut-Check

**Project:** AI-Powered-Personal-Finance-Analyzer
**Created:** 2026-07-08
**Method:** Whiteport Design Studio (WDS)

> **Repurposed scenario:** distinct from Scenario 01's first-touch, exploratory Copilot conversation. This captures Priya as a habitual return user, days later, using the Copilot for a real spending decision.

---

## Transaction (Q1)

**What this scenario covers:**
Days into using the app, facing a real spending decision (a dinner invite), Priya reopens the Copilot for a fast, honest gut-check.

---

## Business Goal (Q2)

**Goal:** 🚀 GROWTH & MONETIZATION — Form the daily briefing habit + convert trust into sustainable revenue
**Objective:** Briefing/return-usage habit (open rate ≥ 70%, Day-7 retention ≥ 4/5); the trust depth that eventually drives conversion — proves the Copilot's value on habitual return use, not just first-time curiosity

---

## User & Situation (Q3)

**Persona:** Priya — The Overwhelmed Earner (Primary)
**Situation:** About two weeks into using the app, during a short break between meetings at her desk, a colleague pings her on Slack to invite her to dinner that will cost around ₹1,200.

---

## Driving Forces (Q4)

**Hope:** Get an instant, honest answer so she can just say yes and enjoy the evening.

**Worry:** Guessing wrong — either overspending into next week's cushion, or needlessly declining out of unfounded fear.

---

## Device & Starting Point (Q5 + Q6)

**Device:** Desktop (localhost, single-user browser app)
**Entry:** Already a habitual user, she opens the app directly to the Copilot in a quick break at her desk, right after getting the invite over Slack.

---

## Best Outcome (Q7)

**User Success:**
A five-second, guilt-free "yes, that's fine" answer with reasoning attached — she enjoys the dinner without lingering doubt.

**Business Success:**
Proves the Copilot's return-usage value — the depth behind habit formation and Day-7 retention (≥4/5), and eventually conversion.

---

## Shortest Path (Q8)

1. **Copilot Chat** — she clicks the "Can I afford ₹___?" gut-check quick prompt, enters ₹1,200, and gets an honest yes with reasoning in two clicks ✓

---

## Trigger Map Connections
