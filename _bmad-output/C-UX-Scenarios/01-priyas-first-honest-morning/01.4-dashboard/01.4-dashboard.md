## Overview

**Page Purpose:** Priya sees her first honest Safe-to-Spend briefing — the hero number, the plain-language "why," her Confidence Score, and everything below the fold as supporting evidence — and understands it without help.

**Entry Context:** Arrives here immediately after reviewing her transactions on 01.3-transactions-table.

**Exit Action:** Having absorbed the briefing, she continues on to see what proactive insights the app has noticed about her spending.

**On-Page Interactions:**
- Reads the Safe-to-Spend hero card (today + after-income figures, with the freshness caveat and one-sentence reasoning)
- Notes her Confidence Score chip
- Reads the plain-language morning briefing narration
- Optionally scrolls to charts/commitments timeline below the fold (supporting evidence, not required for scenario success)
