## Overview

**Page Purpose:** Show Priya that the app has noticed a real, named pattern in her spending — and present it as an observation with evidence, not an accusation or a generic financial tip — so she feels understood, not judged.

**User Situation:** Priya just absorbed her Safe-to-Spend number on the Dashboard. She's still on her first session, still cautiously building trust. She tapped "See my insights" with genuine curiosity — the honest briefing made her think: *"What else does it know about me?"* She's open, but one shame-inducing comment will close her off permanently.

**Success Criteria:** Priya reads at least one insight, recognises it as a real, accurately described pattern in her own spending, and feels understood and informed — not told off. She exits to the Copilot intrigued, not defensive.

**Entry Points:**
- "See my insights" CTA from 01.5 Dashboard

**Exit Points:**
- "Ask the Copilot about this" → **01.7 Copilot Chat** (with pre-populated context)
- Left sidebar: back to Dashboard, or to Transactions/Copilot (Commitments is reached via the Dashboard's "+ Add a commitment" CTA, not the sidebar)

## Page States

| State | When | Appearance | Actions |
|-------|------|------------|---------|
| Loading | Insights generating server-side | Skeleton cards with pulse animation | Wait |
| Default (insights ready) | 1+ insight available | Insight cards visible, footer note if < 3 months data | Read, ask Copilot, dismiss |
| Card dismissed | User tapped "Got it" on a card | Card fades to muted state (still visible, not removed) | Un-dismiss (tap card) |
| All dismissed | All cards dismissed | "Nothing new right now — come back after uploading next month's statement" empty state | Upload statement |
| Insufficient data | < 30 transactions in the statement | Partial insights with data-sufficiency caveat on each card | Upload more data |
| Empty — first visit, insufficient data | Statement too short for any patterns | Empty state: "We need at least a month of transactions to spot patterns. Your first insights will appear once we have more data." | Upload new statement |

## Technical Notes

- **Observation → Evidence → Explanation → Action is the spec** — every insight must hit all four layers. A one-sentence "tip" without evidence is not an insight; it's generic advice and will destroy trust
- **Named patterns, not generic labels** — "Post-payday spike" is a name Priya will recognise and remember; "high spending" is not
- **Evidence must be exact data** — invented or approximated figures break the honesty moat immediately. Every number in the evidence block must be sourced from the transaction data
- **"Information not advice" boundary** — the action layer may suggest *what to consider*, but must not *prescribe* (e.g., "You might want to check if this subscription is still useful" ≠ "Cancel this subscription"). SEBI Investment Adviser boundary applies.
- **Pre-populated Copilot context** — when Priya taps "Ask the Copilot about this", the insight's pattern name + ID must be passed as context so the Copilot can immediately give a relevant, personalised response without Priya having to re-explain
