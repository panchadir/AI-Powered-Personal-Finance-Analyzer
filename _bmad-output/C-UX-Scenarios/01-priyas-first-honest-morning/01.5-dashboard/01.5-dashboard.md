## Overview

**Page Purpose:** Deliver Priya's first honest Safe-to-Spend briefing — the hero number, the plain-language "why," and her Financial Confidence Score — in a way she understands unaided, without feeling judged or overwhelmed.

**User Situation:** Priya just validated her transactions. She's moved past the setup. Now she wants the answer to the question that brought her here: *"Am I okay?"* She's anxious but hopeful. She's already seen that the app is honest about uncertainty — so she's ready to believe the number if it's presented honestly.

**Success Criteria:** Priya sees her Safe-to-Spend number, reads the one-sentence "why," understands the Confidence Score without explanation, and continues to Insights feeling informed and calmer — not more anxious — than when she arrived. WoZ gate: she can correctly explain the Safe-to-Spend number without prompting.

**Entry Points:**
- "See my Dashboard" from 01.4 Transactions Table
- Main nav (recurring access, post-first-visit)

**Exit Points:**
- "See my insights" → **01.6 AI Insights & Recommendations**
- Bottom nav: Transactions, Commitments, Copilot (post-first-visit navigation)

Hero card above the fold dominates. Below the fold: morning briefing narration, then supporting evidence (spending chart, commitments timeline). The fold is intentional — the hero number must land before Priya sees more detail.

## Page States

| State | When | Appearance | Actions |
|-------|------|------------|---------|
| First-visit loading | Just arrived from Transactions Table, dashboard data computing | Hero skeleton + pulse animation; "Calculating your Safe-to-Spend…" label | Wait (typically < 2s) |
| Default (data loaded) | Normal state | Hero card, briefing, insights CTA all visible | Explore, expand "Why", tap insights |
| Why expanded | User tapped "Why ₹amount?" | Inline reasoning block visible below confidence row | Read, collapse |
| Confidence tooltip | User tapped confidence chip | Tooltip popover over chip | Read, dismiss |
| Recurring visit (fresh data) | User returns, new data available | "Updated today" timestamp on hero freshness caveat | Normal interactions |
| Stale data warning | Statement older than 30 days | Amber banner above hero: "This is based on data from {date} — upload a new statement to refresh" | Upload new statement |
| Low confidence state | Confidence = Low | Red-amber chip; confidence tooltip auto-shows on first visit with this state | Read explanation |
| Error — data unavailable | Backend failed to compute Safe-to-Spend | Error card: "We couldn't calculate your Safe-to-Spend right now. We're looking into it." | Retry, contact |

## Technical Notes

- **Two-layer Safe-to-Spend** — "today" and "after salary" are both required on the hero; presenting only one obscures the forward-looking signal that reduces end-of-month anxiety
- **Confidence Score cold-start** — on first upload (1 month of data), confidence will always be Medium or Low. This is expected and should be communicated positively: "Confidence grows with more data" — not as a failure state
- **Briefing text tone constraints** — the AI prompt must be hardcoded with the observation framing rule (no judgment language); this is the honesty moat, not a nice-to-have
- **Below-fold sections are opt-in** — the chart and commitments timeline exist for engaged users; most WoZ participants won't scroll. Don't surface them above the fold.
