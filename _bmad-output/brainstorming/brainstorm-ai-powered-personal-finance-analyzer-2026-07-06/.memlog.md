- (technique) started Jobs To Be Done
- (insight by user) Emotional job: transform financial confusion into financial confidence — replace anxiety/guilt/overwhelm/reactivity with clarity, control, peace of mind, empowerment
- (insight by user) Struggling moment 1 'Payday Uncertainty': after payday+rent+bills, no idea how much is safe to spend for the month; wants 'you're safe to spend this much, and here's why'
- (insight by user) Struggling moment 2 'End-of-Month Shock': last week, shocked at low balance, can't tell what caused overspend across scattered transactions; wants clear where-it-went + habit diagnosis + what to change
- (insight by user) Struggling moment 3 'Upcoming Expenses Anxiety': can't remember/estimate all near-future bills (rent, utilities, insurance, birthday); wants look-ahead prediction + reassurance or early warning
- (decision by user) CORE PRINCIPLE: every AI insight follows Observation -> Evidence -> Explanation -> Action; tone honest/empathetic/non-judgmental so user feels understood, not judged (behavioral mirror, not category report)
- (idea by user) Behavioral insight: stress/exhaustion spending (delivery+convenience spikes Sun eves & after busy workdays)
- (idea by user) Behavioral insight: death-by-small-purchases (frequent small buys outweigh occasional big ones)
- (idea by user) Behavioral insight: post-payday spending spike (first-week overspend leaves no late-month flex)
- (idea by user) Behavioral insight: zombie subscriptions (recurring memberships with low recent usage/value)
- (idea by user) Behavioral insight: repeating monthly cycle (comfortable start -> fast 2-week spend -> cautious month-end)
- (idea by user) Behavioral insight: 'surprises' aren't surprises (insurance/annual subs/family events recur predictably)
- (idea by user) Behavioral insight: real risk is lack of buffer, not overspend — little emergency room; prioritize modest safety buffer
- (technique) started How Might We
- (question by user) HMW1: proactively warn of shortfalls BEFORE they happen, with time to adjust (not react after damage)
- (question by user) HMW2: surface hidden habits users can't see themselves, presented supportively/non-judgmentally to drive positive change
- (question by user) HMW3: turn complex transactions into simple personalized insights (where money goes & why) w/o financial expertise
- (question by user) HMW4: balance today's spend vs tomorrow's commitments via real-time AI safe-to-spend guidance that keeps savings goals on track
- (idea by user) CONCEPT 1 'AI Financial Copilot': app opens to a conversation, not charts; daily briefing gives safe-to-spend number + plain-language WHY (rent covered, bill due in 5d, emergency fund untouched, savings on track). Trust via explained reasoning
- (idea by user) CONCEPT 2 'Financial Timeline (Google Maps for Money)': visualize the financial FUTURE as a timeline of upcoming inflows/outflows; AI continuously simulates cash flow; every purchase instantly recalcs safe-to-spend & confirms upcoming commitments still covered
- (idea by user) CONCEPT 3 'Financial Guardian (proactive intervention)': AI quietly monitors, speaks up only when helpful — pace warnings ('short by ~2800 before payday'), gentle nudges, purchase reassurance. Never criticizes: informs/reassures/suggests
- (idea by user) CONCEPT 4 'Financial Confidence Score (0-100)': dynamic score of PREPAREDNESS not wealth (upcoming bills, spending pace, income stability, savings progress, emergency buffer, recurring commitments); AI explains every score change +/-
- (decision by user) 'The Trust Loop' = signature loyalty mechanic: Prediction -> Explanation -> Recommendation -> Outcome, applied to every rec so users understand->trust->rely. (extends O->E->E->A principle)
- (decision by user) PILLAR 'Confidence with Humility': every rec carries Recommendation + Reasoning + Confidence%. Under uncertainty AI gets MORE transparent/conservative/collaborative, never silent. Core moat: honesty incumbents can't easily copy ('trust it because it tells me when it doesn't know')
- (insight by user) Failure mode - irregular/variable income: use conservative estimate, label it as unconfirmed, auto-increase safe-to-spend when income arrives
- (insight by user) Failure mode - unexpected one-off (medical/repair): don't pretend it was predictable; recalc outlook & give adaptive daily adjustment. Adapt, don't apologize
- (insight by user) Failure mode - missing data (cash/multi-bank/shared): disclose visibility limits ('based on connected accounts; cash/other accounts may shift this')
- (insight by user) Failure mode - variable recurring bills: reserve conservative top-of-range, show the range, auto-release spending if actual is lower
- (insight by user) Failure mode - low prediction confidence: surface confidence % + WHY it's lower + what extra data would improve accuracy (turn uncertainty into a collaboration prompt)
- (technique) started Build on What Works
- (decision by user) DIFFERENTIATION THESIS: absorb YNAB's 'give every rupee a job' philosophy but remove the manual effort. AI auto-infers allocations from recurring bills/commitments/habits/goals/cash-flow forecasts. Positioning: 'Every Rupee Already Has a Job — Your AI Is Managing It for You' — budgeting app -> AI financial copilot; confidence without constant effort
- (insight by user) Why incumbents leave the gap: YNAB's zero-based budgeting demands time/discipline/financial literacy -> high abandonment; the AI-native win is making the SAME mindset effortless & always-explained
- (decision by user) DATA PHILOSOPHY: AI adapts to user's financial life, not vice versa. Multi-source & user picks automation level: connected bank APIs, CSV/PDF statement upload (AI extracts+categorizes+explains), receipt scan (merchant/items), manual cash entry (AI learns categories), recurring-commitment setup for forecasts
- (idea by user) 'Every input creates immediate value': AI never just thanks user — it shows what changed (found 3 subs, recalced safe-to-spend, improved grocery forecast). Loop: User Action -> AI Learns -> User Sees Immediate Benefit
- (idea by user) AI learns from corrections: recategorization ('Dining or Groceries?') teaches merchant rules for future -> explains how the fix improves future recs (human-in-the-loop learning)
- (idea by user) Data provenance transparency: every rec lists exactly what it used (2 accounts, 1 statement, 4 bills, 3 cash, savings goal) AND what's missing (no cash logged this week -> add to improve accuracy)
- (insight) SYNTHESIS: (1) Confidence-with-Humility is what earns the right to auto-remove YNAB's manual effort — transparency buys the automation's buy-in. (2) Confidence Score is the product spine: renders behavioral insights + forecast + %confidence as one number-that-goes-up (retention+emotion+honesty). (3) The failure-mode handling (freelancer/cash/variable bills) is the real moat incumbents can't copy without breaking their 'always right' brand. (4) 'Surprises aren't surprises' powers the proactive Guardian. Core product = AI Financial Copilot organized around Behind/Now/Ahead, bound by Trust Loop.
