# Business Goals & Objectives

> Strategic goals and measurable objectives for the AI-Powered Personal Finance Analyzer (AI Financial Copilot)

**Document:** Trigger Map — Business Goals
**Created:** 2026-07-07
**Status:** COMPLETE

---

## Vision

**Become the most trusted daily financial voice for financially-anxious salaried Indians — the AI copilot that measurably turns money dread into calm confidence every morning, and never once causes harm through a wrong number.**

We are not building an expense tracker. We are building the thing that finally feels *better than looking away*. When Priya opens her phone at 8am, the first thing she hears is not a red alert or a guilt-tripping chart — it is a calm, honest voice telling her exactly how much she can safely spend today, and why. Over 30 days, her self-reported anxiety measurably drops. That is the whole game.

---

## Business Objectives

### ⭐ PRIMARY GOAL: Measurably Reduce Financial Anxiety (THE ENGINE)

- **Statement:** Create awesome, calm users whose self-reported financial anxiety measurably decreases after 30 days of daily briefings — proven first in a Wizard-of-Oz (WoZ) pilot before any scaled build.
- **Metric:** Self-reported daily anxiety rating (captured at onboarding, tracked to Day 30) + Safe-to-Spend comprehension.
- **Target:** WoZ gate — average daily anxiety rating **≥ 3.5/5**; **5 of 5** pilot users correctly understand the Safe-to-Spend number; measurable anxiety decrease for the majority of the cohort by Day 30.
- **Timeline:** WoZ validation within **~3 months**; result is the go/no-go gate for the Phase 2 build.
- **Impact:** This is THE ENGINE. A calm Priya checks in daily, trusts the AI, tells her friends, and eventually pays. Every other objective below is *driven by* winning this one. If anxiety does not drop, nothing else matters — we return to problem definition.

**Kill signal (non-negotiable):** a single Safe-to-Spend error causing real financial harm (e.g. an overdraft or a missed committed obligation) triggers an immediate feature pause and trust audit before relaunch. Conservative-by-default calculation is a hard requirement, not a preference.

---

### 🚀 GROWTH & MONETIZATION OBJECTIVES (Driven by calm, trusting users)

**Objective 1: Form the daily briefing habit**
- **Statement:** Turn the morning Safe-to-Spend briefing into a checked-daily habit — the behavioral foundation everything else stands on.
- **Metric:** Daily briefing open rate; early-cohort retention.
- **Target:** Briefing open rate **≥ 70%**; WoZ Day-7 retention **≥ 4 of 5** users still checking. D30 retention treated as an *aspirational stretch* (category baseline is ~4.2%) requiring an explicit habit strategy, not a default gate.
- **Timeline:** Day-7 signal within the 3-month WoZ; open-rate target ongoing from launch.

**Objective 2: Convert trust into sustainable revenue**
- **Statement:** Convert habit and trust into paid subscriptions, while actively testing a revenue-diversification path beyond pure subscription.
- **Metric:** Free→Paid conversion at 30 days (₹199 / ₹499 tiers).
- **Target:** Test the ₹199/₹499 price points and a **1-5%** conversion band (India/SEA is the lowest-converting region globally — 5% is the optimistic edge, to be validated in WoZ, not assumed).
- **Timeline:** First conversion read at the WoZ/pilot stage; diversification hypothesis defined *before* scaling spend (subscription-only caps ~$100M globally).

**Objective 3: Grow through advocacy, not ad spend**
- **Statement:** Let calm, awesome users naturally become champions who refer friends — keeping CAC near zero during validation.
- **Metric:** Referral rate among active users.
- **Target:** **≥ 30%** of active users referring at least one friend (well-supported: 34.92% of Indian users discover finance apps via friend referral; 83% trust a personal recommendation over ads).
- **Timeline:** Measured from the first paid cohort onward.

### 🌟 ECOSYSTEM & MEMBER-BENEFIT OBJECTIVES (Real-world outcomes for users and sponsors)

**Note:** These are the real-world benefits the product creates *for* its users and for the employers who may later sponsor it — the outcomes that make the flywheel worth spinning.

**Objective 4: Make users genuinely more prepared, not just calmer**
- **Statement:** Move users from financially fragile to financially prepared — the honest outcome behind the calm.
- **Metric:** Financial Confidence Score trend (0-100, preparedness not wealth); self-reported emergency-readiness.
- **Target:** Confidence Score trending **up for ≥ 80%** of users in their first 30 days (only 26% of the target population feel emergency-ready today).
- **Timeline:** First 30 days per user, sustained thereafter.
- **Benefit to Members:** Users stop living in end-of-month shock and payday uncertainty; they build a real buffer and know it.

**Objective 5: Prove a quantified employer retention incentive (Year 2 B2B2C amplifier)**
- **Statement:** Give employers a measurable reason to sponsor the copilot for their people — financial calm as a retention lever.
- **Metric:** Employer-sponsored cohort anxiety reduction + engagement, framed against retention/productivity.
- **Target:** Demonstrable anxiety reduction in a sponsored pilot cohort (financial anxiety correlates with ~40% higher job-search likelihood — the retention math employers care about).
- **Timeline:** **Year 2**, only after consumer PMF is proven. Not a Phase 1 distraction.
- **Benefit to Members:** Employees get a genuinely useful benefit; employers get quantified retention — a rare win-win.

## Regulatory & Trust Guardrails (constrain every objective above)

- All output framed as **"information the user's own data shows," never "advice"** (SEBI Investment Adviser boundary; legal opinion required before ~10,000 users).
- Operate as / partner as a regulated **FIU** to receive AA-consented data; **DPDP Rule 4** consent architecture built to the Nov-2026 standard now, not retrofitted.
- **Multi-source ingestion at MVP** (only ~38% of borrowers are AA-enabled) — manual entry, CSV/PDF, receipt scan are load-bearing, not v2.
- **Data-privacy-as-differentiator** — lead with "here's what we do NOT share" (52% of Indian users already fear their data is sold).
