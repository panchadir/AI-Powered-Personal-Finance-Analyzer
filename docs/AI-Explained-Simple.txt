===============================================================================
 HOW THIS APP USES AI - EXPLAINED SIMPLY
===============================================================================

 This app uses an AI (Claude) in 4 places. This note explains each one in plain
 English, so anyone can understand it - no technical background needed.


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 THE ONE THING TO REMEMBER FIRST
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

 The app does ALL of your money math itself, like a calculator. The AI NEVER
 makes up or calculates your numbers.

 The AI only helps in two simple ways:
     1. SORTING   - putting a label on things (like filing receipts).
     2. EXPLAINING - writing your already-calculated numbers into friendly,
                     easy-to-read sentences.

 So the numbers you see are always the real, calculated numbers. The AI just
 helps organise and describe them.


===============================================================================
 THE 4 PLACES THE AI IS USED
===============================================================================


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 1. SORTING YOUR TRANSACTIONS   (when you upload a bank statement)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

 WHAT YOU DO:
     You upload your bank statement.

 WHAT HAPPENS:
     The app first sorts your transactions using a simple built-in list of
     well-known names. For example, it already knows:
         "Swiggy" or "Zomato"  -> Food & Dining
         "Netflix"             -> Entertainment
         "HDFC EMI"            -> Loan Repayment
     Most transactions get sorted instantly this way, with NO AI needed.

     Only the FEW leftover ones it doesn't recognise are shown to the AI, which
     reads the name and suggests the right category.

 SIMPLE ANALOGY:
     Like sorting a pile of receipts. The obvious ones you file yourself in
     seconds. Only the confusing few do you hand to a knowledgeable assistant
     and ask, "which folder does this go in?"

 DOES IT ALWAYS USE AI?
     No. If the app recognises every transaction on its own, the AI is not
     used at all. It's only called for the unknown leftovers - and even then,
     all of them are sent together in ONE quick request (to keep it cheap).


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 2. THE MORNING SUMMARY   (when you open your Dashboard)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

 WHAT YOU DO:
     You open your Dashboard.

 WHAT HAPPENS:
     The app calculates your figures first - how much is safe to spend, what's
     set aside for bills, and so on. Then it hands those finished numbers to the
     AI and asks it to write a short, calm 2-3 sentence summary.

     Example the AI might write:
         "You have Rs 2,840 safe to spend today, after we set aside Rs 22,200
          for the bills you've committed to. This is based on your statement up
          to 30 Jun 2026."

     Every number in that sentence is copied from the app's own calculation -
     the AI is not allowed to change or invent any figure.

 SIMPLE ANALOGY:
     Like a helpful friend glancing at your finished bank summary and saying it
     back to you in a warm, easy sentence - instead of you reading a wall of
     numbers.

 DOES IT ALWAYS USE AI?
     Yes - each time you open the Dashboard, it writes a fresh summary.
     (If the AI is switched off, the app shows a plain built-in summary instead,
     so the page is never blank.)


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 3. YOUR SPENDING INSIGHTS   (when you open the Insights page)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

 WHAT YOU DO:
     You open the Insights page.

 WHAT HAPPENS  (this one is often misunderstood):
     The app itself - NOT the AI - studies your spending and FINDS the patterns.
     For example, it detects things like:
         - your spending spiked right after payday
         - a subscription you seem to have forgotten about
         - lots of small purchases adding up
         - a big bill coming up that clashes with your balance

     Finding these patterns is done by the app's own logic. The AI is NOT
     involved in figuring them out.

     THEN, once the app has found a pattern, it asks the AI to write it up as a
     friendly, easy-to-read note (what we noticed, the evidence, why it matters,
     and a gentle suggestion).

 SIMPLE ANALOGY:
     The app is the detective who finds the clues. The AI is the writer who
     turns the detective's findings into a clear, readable story. The AI never
     does the detective work - it only writes up what was already found.

 DOES IT ALWAYS USE AI?
     No. Once a note is written, it is SAVED. The AI is only asked again when a
     brand-new pattern appears, or an existing one's numbers change noticeably
     (for example, after you upload a new statement). Nothing new? No AI call -
     it just reuses the saved note.


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 4. THE CHAT ASSISTANT (COPILOT)   (when you ask a question)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

 WHAT YOU DO:
     You type a question, like "Can I afford Rs 5,000 this weekend?"

 WHAT HAPPENS:
     The AI answers you - but it is not allowed to guess your numbers. When it
     needs a figure, it looks it up from your real data first (safe, read-only
     look-ups the app provides), and only then answers. The reply appears
     word-by-word as it types.

     It also shows little "Based on:" tags so you can see exactly which of your
     real data it used.

 REMEMBERING THE CONVERSATION:
     The AI has no memory of its own. So how does it follow the conversation?
     Each time you ask something, the app quietly re-sends the WHOLE chat so far
     (all your earlier questions and its earlier answers) along with your new
     question. That's how it "remembers" - the app keeps the memory for it.

 YOUR VERY FIRST QUESTION:
     On your first question there's no earlier chat to include - so it just
     sends that one question. But it is never clueless: the app always sends its
     built-in instructions (its personality and honesty rules) and gives it
     access to your data look-up tools. From the second question onward, the
     earlier conversation is added on.

 SIMPLE ANALOGY:
     Like texting an assistant who forgets everything between messages. To keep
     them up to speed, you paste the whole conversation each time before your
     new question. They can also look things up in your file cabinet (but only
     READ it, never change it) before replying.

 DOES IT ALWAYS USE AI?
     Yes - every question you ask goes to the AI. For one question it may make
     two quick trips: one to decide what data to look up, and one to write the
     final answer using that data.
