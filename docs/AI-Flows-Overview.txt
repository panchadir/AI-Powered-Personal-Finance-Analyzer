===============================================================================
 HOW THE APP USES AI - SIMPLE OVERVIEW
===============================================================================

 The app uses an AI model (called Claude) in 4 places. This note explains how
 each one works - simply, but with just enough of the "how" so it makes sense.
 No code, no jargon dumps.

 A few words used below, explained once:
   - AI model    = Claude, the AI that reads/writes text.
   - Rules engine= a small built-in program that matches keywords (no AI).
   - Database    = where your data is stored (transactions, insights, chats...).
   - Tools       = small helpers that FETCH your data (read-only: look, not change).

 THE GOLDEN RULE:
   The app's own code does all the money MATH and all the pattern-FINDING.
   The AI model only SORTS things and WRITES friendly text. It never invents
   your numbers.


===============================================================================
 1. SORTING TRANSACTIONS  (when you upload a statement)
===============================================================================

 WHEN: you upload your bank statement.

 HOW IT WORKS:
   1. The rules engine checks each transaction against a built-in keyword list
      (Swiggy -> Food & Dining, Netflix -> Entertainment, etc.), plus any
      corrections you've taught it before. This step uses NO AI.
   2. Anything it recognises is labelled instantly. The few it doesn't
      recognise are marked "Uncategorized".
   3. All the uncategorized ones are bundled together and sent in ONE request
      to the AI model, along with the fixed list of allowed categories.
   4. The AI picks a category for each and sends them back. Everything is saved
      to the database.

 WHAT IS SENT TO THE AI:
   Only the DESCRIPTION text of the unknown transactions - not the amounts,
   dates, or balances.

 GOOD TO KNOW:
   If the rules engine recognises everything, the AI is never called. And the
   AI can only choose from the fixed category list, so it can't invent a
   strange category.


===============================================================================
 2. THE DASHBOARD SUMMARY  (when you open your Dashboard)
===============================================================================

 WHEN: you open the Dashboard.

 HOW IT WORKS:
   1. The app calculates your figures first - safe-to-spend, money reserved for
      bills, and so on. This math is done by the app, not the AI.
   2. It puts those finished numbers into a short "facts" list and sends it to
      the AI model, asking for a calm 2-3 sentence summary.
   3. The AI writes the summary using ONLY those numbers and sends the text
      back. It appears at the top of your Dashboard.

 WHAT IS SENT TO THE AI:
   Only the already-calculated figures - and the AI is told it may not change
   or add any number.

 GOOD TO KNOW:
   This runs every time you open the Dashboard. If the AI is turned off (or has
   no key), the app shows a simple built-in summary instead, so the page is
   never blank.


===============================================================================
 3. SPENDING INSIGHTS  (when you open the Insights page)
===============================================================================

 WHEN: you open the Insights page.
 (This one is the most misunderstood - read carefully.)

 HOW IT WORKS:
   1. The app's own detector logic scans your transactions and commitments and
      FINDS the patterns - e.g. "spending spiked after payday", "a forgotten
      subscription", "a big bill clashes with your balance". *** This is done by
      the app, NOT by the AI. ***
   2. For each pattern it found, the app sends just that pattern's facts to the
      AI model, asking it to write a short, friendly note about it.
   3. The AI writes the note and it is SAVED in the database.
   4. Next time you visit, saved notes are reused. The AI is only asked again
      for a brand-new pattern, or one whose numbers changed noticeably.

 WHAT IS SENT TO THE AI:
   Only the pattern's facts (what was found and the evidence) - NOT your raw
   transaction list.

 GOOD TO KNOW:
   The AI does not decide what the insights are or work out any number. It only
   writes up findings the app already made. Think of the app as the detective
   and the AI as the writer.


===============================================================================
 4. THE CHAT ASSISTANT / COPILOT  (when you ask a question)
===============================================================================

 WHEN: you type a question, e.g. "Can I afford Rs 5,000 this weekend?"

 HOW IT WORKS:
   1. The app builds the message to send: your earlier questions and answers,
      then your new question - plus its built-in instructions and a set of
      read-only tools.
   2. It sends this to the AI model. The AI may reply, "I need real data - run
      these tools."
   3. The app runs those tools, which FETCH your real figures from the database
      (they can only read your data, never change it), and sends the results
      back to the AI.
   4. The AI writes the final answer using those real numbers. It appears
      word-by-word as it types, with small "Based on:" tags showing which data
      it used.
   5. The question and answer are saved to the database.

 ABOUT MEMORY:
   The AI has no memory of its own. So every time you ask something, the app
   re-sends the WHOLE conversation so far, plus the new question. That is how it
   "remembers" - the app keeps the memory, not the AI.

 YOUR FIRST QUESTION:
   The first time, there's no earlier conversation - so only your one question
   is sent. But the AI is never clueless: the app ALWAYS sends its built-in
   instructions and the data tools. From the second question on, the earlier
   chat is added.

 GOOD TO KNOW:
   Because it looks up real data instead of guessing, the AI cannot make up your
   figures. One question may take two quick trips to the AI: one to decide which
   data to fetch, and one to write the answer.
